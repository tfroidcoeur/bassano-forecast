Long range — ensemble, low confidence

ECMWF ensemble at the launch point, pooled over its members. Rain, wind and foehn use 09–17 local time, temperature the whole day. Wind: median with p10–p90 and the mean direction. Rain: share of members with at least 1 mm. North flow: share of member-hours at least 15 km/h from within 60° of north at 850 or 700 hPa. Foehn: Bozen−Innsbruck, member minimum, p10 / p50 / p90. From day 6 on (faded) the forecast is less reliable.

Foehn index, 14 days, full detail

Bozen−Innsbruck is the sea-level pressure in South Tyrol minus that in Innsbruck, in hPa. Negative values mean a north-south gradient over the Alps, i.e. north foehn in South Tyrol. Classes: under 2 hPa none, 2 to 3 weak, 3 to 6 moderate, over 6 strong. The shaded bands mark 3 and 6 hPa. The day class uses the most negative of ECMWF and ICON between 09 and 17 local time.
